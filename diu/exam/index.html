IMPORTANT EXAM INSTRUCTIONS

  1. Question Categories: There are two different question categories, each represented by a different color based on the assigned column. The corresponding color will be displayed on the screen.
  2. Tab Switching Prohibited: Any attempt to switch tabs or navigate away from the exam screen will be detected. Students attempting to do so will be immediately expelled from the exam.
  3. Electronic Devices and Notes Prohibited: Students are strictly prohibited from using mobile phones, smartwatches, or any electronic devices other than the assigned lab PC. The use of notebooks or written notes is also not allowed.
  4. Countdown Timer and Submission: A countdown timer will be displayed in the top-right corner of the screen. Students must complete and submit their exams within the allocated time.
  5. Viva Examination: Each student will receive different viva questions. Students are encouraged to express their answers freely and naturally, just as they would in a real conversation.
